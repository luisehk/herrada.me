An open archive of the trees of Mexico.

the complex challenge
Every tree of Mexico, with a source behind every fact.
the simple answer
One sheet per species, read from printed field guides page by page. What is not verified prints [pendiente], the archive's word for pending.

Every species gets a sheet: its scientific name, the names people use where it grows, its height drawn next to a person, where it has been found and the months it flowers. Every fact cites the page it came from.

how I work

a balanced approach

From 2016 to 2019 I ran Vordem, a digital product agency. Its home page had a heading I still work by: “A balanced approach.” Strategists, developers, designers and product managers on one problem, looking for the simple answer. Today agents hold some of those seats. One shapes the work with me. One argues with the design before any code exists. One builds. A panel of reviewers reads the result and never sees the plan. The decisions stay with me.

I work in a terminal: i3, tmux, vim. Every project I own has one command that has to pass before anything ships, and a written record of why things are the way they are. That holds for the real-estate platform and for the tiny games.

  • One command gates every repo. Nothing ships that does not pass.
  • I write down why. More than 250 decision records across seven projects, ten of them for thirteen tiny games.
  • Status is stated as it is. Pre-launch means pre-launch.
  • Machines check the work. Every vim puzzle is replayed in CI. Every game is replayed from a seed.
