Field notes

arbopedia is an open archive of the trees of Mexico. Every species gets a sheet: its scientific name, the names people use where it grows, its height drawn next to a person, where it has been found and the months it flowers. Every fact cites the page it came from. What is not verified prints [pendiente].

  • 2023 The first version.
  • July to September 2026 Rebuilt as an open archive with its own design system.
  • 2026-11-25 396 sheets, 1,421 pages read across 10 printed works.

Checked against GBIF, the IUCN Red List and POWO. Indigenous names are never translated and always carry their language. Colour contrast is recomputed from the CSS on every CI run. One Instagram post a day. Texts are CC BY-SA 4.0.

vim-puzzles is a trainer for vim: 200 short editing puzzles in a real vim emulation in the browser, each scored against par like golf. No accounts and no backend. Progress stays in your browser.

  • 2026-08-20 First commit.
  • October 2026 The corpus reaches 200 puzzles, and the app becomes Smithy’s first outside pilot.

Three stars at or under par. Every solution is replayed by the real engine in CI, so no puzzle ships unless it is solvable at its par. It looks like tmux panes in Solarized Dark because that is what my terminal looks like.

lichess-irl lets me play Lichess on a physical board. A webcam looks straight down, the app reads my opponent’s moves out loud, I make my move on the board and press the space bar like a chess clock. Built for exactly one desk.

  • December 2025 A 3D piece set for the board package behind the Lichess mobile app. A branch on a public fork, not merged upstream.
  • August 2026 lichess-irl.

Rapid games at 15+10. Moves go through the official Lichess Board API. A terminal interface shows the board, both clocks and a running log. The four markers sit just outside the board’s corners, numbered 0 to 3 clockwise from the top left as the camera sees them.

minigames is a family of 13 tiny 2D games in Python: catch, snake, breakout, a platformer, air hockey, a nine-car racer. They share one architecture and one quality gate, and each has its own generated 8-bit tune.

  • 2026-08-06 pyplatformer, the sketch: a window, a cloud and a collision box. It is tree 4 on the plate.
  • 2026-08-14 to 2026-08-21 Thirteen games.

Every shape is a placeholder that can be swapped for a drawing in two lines. The menu gives each game a hook of a few words: “catch what falls”, “grow long, don’t crash”, “thread the gaps”, “run, jump, dash”, “two players, one keyboard”.

About

I’m Luis, a software engineer in Monterrey, Mexico. I have spent 18+ years taking products from the first idea to production, and I still like the simple solution best. Today I build Rentaventa, a real-estate platform for the city’s brokers, with a partner. I also build Smithy, the workshop where AI agents do most of my building and I keep the decisions. The rest is for the love of it: an archive of Mexico’s trees, a vim trainer, chess on a real board, a box of tiny games.

How I work

How I work

From 2016 to 2019 I ran Vordem, a digital product agency. Its home page had a heading I still work by: “A balanced approach.” Strategists, developers, designers and product managers on one problem, looking for the simple answer. Today agents hold some of those seats. One shapes the work with me. One argues with the design before any code exists. One builds. A panel of reviewers reads the result and never sees the plan. The decisions stay with me.

I work in a terminal: i3, tmux, vim. Every project I own has one command that has to pass before anything ships, and a written record of why things are the way they are. That holds for the real-estate platform and for the tiny games.

How I build with agents has the whole method.

Habits

Habits

  • One command gates every repo. Nothing ships that does not pass.
  • I write down why. More than 250 decision records across seven projects, ten of them for thirteen tiny games.
  • Status is stated as it is. Pre-launch means pre-launch.
  • Machines check the work. Every vim puzzle is replayed in CI. Every game is replayed from a seed.
